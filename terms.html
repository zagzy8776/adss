Official Rules

PayoutPool Payout Pool · Last updated 18 November 2026

1. Definitions

1.1 Promotion means the PayoutPool payout pool governed by these Rules and conducted in successive Rounds.

1.2 Sponsor, we, us and our mean PayoutPool, the promoter and administrator of the Promotion.

1.3 Entrant and you mean the individual who submits an Entry.

1.4 Entry means the record created when an Entrant completes the on-page steps and submits an email address and a mobile number.

1.5 Round means an entry period described in clause 4.

1.6 Prize Pool means the aggregate amount disbursed in a Round.

1.7 Winners page means the page at /winners.html on which the results of completed Rounds are published.

1.8 Site means the website on which these Rules are published.

2. The Sponsor

2.1 The Promotion is organised and administered by the Sponsor, which is responsible for its operation, the verification of Entries, the conduct of the draw and the disbursement of the Prize Pool.

2.2 These Rules govern the Promotion and prevail over any summary, headline, advertisement or other statement published elsewhere, including copy on the Site. Where the copy on the Site and these Rules differ, these Rules apply.

3. Eligibility

3.1 The Promotion is open to individuals aged 18 or over at the time of Entry, worldwide, except where participation in a promotion of this kind would be unlawful under the laws applicable to the Entrant.

3.2 Employees of the Sponsor, its advertising partners and members of their immediate households are not eligible.

3.3 One Entry per person per Round. Duplicate Entries, automated or scripted Entries, and Entries submitted on behalf of another person are void, and may result in exclusion from current and future Rounds.

3.4 An Entrant must be the authorised holder of the email address and mobile number submitted, and must be able to receive messages at that number.

3.5 The Sponsor may require proof of identity, age and eligibility, and may withhold a prize until that proof is provided.

3.6 An Entry submitted by a person under 18 is void on discovery, whether or not a prize has already been awarded.

4. Entry period and Rounds

4.1 The Promotion is conducted in successive Rounds, each of one calendar month.

4.2 A Round opens at 00:00 UTC on the first day of the month and closes at 23:59 UTC on the last day of that month. The times recorded in the Sponsor's administration system are operative.

4.3 An Entry received outside an open Round is not accepted into that Round.

4.4 The Sponsor may postpone, suspend or cancel a Round where it cannot be conducted as planned. Where a Round is cancelled before the draw, no Prize Pool is awarded for that Round.

4.5 The results of completed Rounds, including the number of Winners and the amount disbursed to each, are published on the Winners page.

5. How to enter

5.1 Complete the on-page steps and submit a valid email address and a valid mobile number, including the country code where one is required, and confirm the consent statement.

5.2 An Entry is complete when the confirmation screen is displayed. A submission rejected on validation, or one that fails because a required field is inaccurate, does not create an Entry.

5.3 Optional third-party offers are not part of the entry process. Completing an offer does not create an Entry, does not complete an incomplete Entry and does not improve any Entrant's odds.

5.4 The queue position displayed on the Site represents the order in which Entries are processed for review. It is a display element only: it is not a ranking, it does not express a probability of winning, and it has no effect on the outcome of a draw.

6. Verification and disqualification

6.1 The Sponsor may verify any Entry, including by confirming that the email address and mobile number are valid and reachable.

6.2 The Sponsor may disqualify an Entry, and withhold or recover a prize already released, where it reasonably believes that the Entry was obtained by fraud, automation or tampering, that the Entrant is ineligible, or that these Rules have been breached.

6.3 A disqualified Entry forfeits its position without compensation, and a replacement Winner may be drawn under clause 10.

7. Prize Pool and approximate retail value

7.1 The Prize Pool for each Round has an aggregate approximate retail value of USD 5,000.

7.2 The Prize Pool is disbursed in whole or in part across one or more Winners in the Round. The number of Winners and the amount allocated to each are published on the Winners page after the draw.

7.3 A prize is limited to the amount stated for that Winner. Prizes are non-transferable and are not redeemable for cash beyond the stated amount. No substitution except by the Sponsor, for a prize of equal or greater value, where required.

7.4 Amounts are stated in United States dollars. A Winner outside the United States is paid in USD, or in the equivalent converted at the exchange rate applied on the payout date, at the Sponsor's election.

7.5 Prizes are disbursed by one of the methods listed on the Winners page. A Winner must supply accurate payout details; the Sponsor is not liable for a payment sent to details supplied incorrectly.

7.6 The Sponsor does not request a payment, a deposit, a gift card code or a release fee from a Winner.

8. Odds of winning

8.1 Odds of winning depend on the total number of eligible Entries received in the Round and are not fixed.

8.2 Odds are not improved by completing optional offers, by sharing a link, or by the queue position displayed.

9. Winner determination

9.1 Winners are selected at random from all eligible Entries received in the Round, using a verifiable random method.

9.2 The draw takes place within five business days of the close of the Round.

9.3 The result of the draw is final, subject only to verification of eligibility and to clauses 6 and 10.

10. Notification and claim period

10.1 The Sponsor notifies a Winner by email to the address on the Entry and, where consent was given, by SMS to the number on the Entry.

10.2 A Winner must respond within 72 hours of the first notification, confirm eligibility and sign a release where one is required.

10.3 Where a Winner does not respond within the claim period, cannot be verified, declines the prize, or a notification is returned undeliverable, the prize is forfeited and a replacement Winner is drawn from the remaining eligible Entries.

10.4 The Sponsor is not responsible for a notification that fails to reach a Winner because the contact details supplied were inaccurate, or because of a filtering or messaging failure outside the Sponsor's control.

11. Taxes, documentation and local conditions

11.1 Each Winner is responsible for all taxes, duties and reporting obligations arising on a prize in their place of residence.

11.2 A Winner must provide the tax documentation the Sponsor is required to collect before a prize is released, for example a Form W-9 for a person subject to United States tax.

11.3 Where the law applicable to a Winner requires it, the Winner must answer a time-limited skill-testing question correctly before the prize is released.

12. Publicity

12.1 By accepting a prize, a Winner consents to the Sponsor publishing their first name and last initial, country, Round and prize amount on the Winners page and in related communications for the Promotion.

12.2 No further identifying information is published without the Winner's separate consent.

13. Calls and text messages

13.1 Where the consent box is ticked, the Entrant agrees to receive calls and text messages from the Sponsor about the Entry, including by automated means, at the number supplied.

13.2 Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel or HELP for help.

13.3 Consent is not a condition of entry or of winning, and opting out of messages does not withdraw an Entry.

14. Personal information

14.1 Information submitted is collected, used and retained as described in the Privacy Policy, which forms part of these Rules.

14.2 The Sponsor uses the email address and mobile number to administer the Entry, verify eligibility and contact a Winner about a payout. Personal information is not sold.

14.3 Entries are retained for 24 months after the close of the Round in which they were received and are then deleted, save where a longer period is required by law.

14.4 Entrants may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their information in accordance with the Privacy Policy.

15. Advertising and third parties

15.1 The Site displays advertising and sponsored placements, served by third parties which may set cookies or use device identifiers as described in the Privacy Policy.

15.2 Advertising has no effect on the draw. Clicking an advertisement is not required in order to enter and does not create an Entry.

15.3 Optional offers are operated by third parties under their own terms. The Sponsor is not a party to those offers and does not control their availability, fulfilment or payment.

16. Limitation of liability

16.1 To the fullest extent permitted by law, the Sponsor is not liable for: (a) late, lost, misdirected, incomplete or illegible Entries; (b) unauthorised human or technical intervention in the Promotion; (c) the failure of any network, device or third-party service; (d) any act or omission of a third party, including an advertising network, a payment provider or an offer partner; (e) a Winner's failure to receive a notification for a reason outside the Sponsor's control.

16.2 Nothing in these Rules excludes or limits liability that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited, including liability for fraud, or for death or personal injury caused by negligence.

17. Indemnity

17.1 To the extent permitted by law, an Entrant indemnifies the Sponsor against claims, losses and reasonable costs arising from that Entrant's breach of these Rules or unlawful conduct in connection with the Promotion.

18. Governing law and disputes

18.1 These Rules are governed by the law of the jurisdiction in which the Sponsor is established, without regard to conflict-of-law rules.

18.2 The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of that jurisdiction, save that nothing in this clause deprives an Entrant of the protection of mandatory consumer law in their place of residence, including any right to bring proceedings there.

18.3 Before commencing proceedings, a party must give the other written notice of the dispute and allow 30 days for resolution.

18.4 The outcome of a draw is not open to challenge save on the ground of manifest error or bad faith.

19. General

19.1 If a provision of these Rules is held invalid or unenforceable, it is severed and the remaining provisions continue in full effect.

19.2 A failure or delay in enforcing a provision is not a waiver of it.

19.3 These Rules, the Privacy Policy and the Winners page constitute the entire agreement between the Entrant and the Sponsor in respect of the Promotion.

19.4 The Sponsor may amend these Rules to comply with law or to correct a manifest error. An amendment applies to Rounds that open after it is published, unless the amendment is required by law.

19.5 The Sponsor may suspend, cancel or modify the Promotion where a virus, bug, unauthorised intervention or any other cause outside its control corrupts or impairs its administration.

19.6 The Sponsor may assign its rights and obligations under these Rules to a successor entity operating the Promotion.

19.7 A person who is not a party to these Rules has no right to enforce them.

19.8 The English version of these Rules prevails over any translation.

20. Acceptance and contact

20.1 By submitting an Entry, an Entrant accepts these Rules and the Privacy Policy.

20.2 Questions about these Rules, an Entry or a Round: support@YOURDOMAIN.com.
