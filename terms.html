Terms of participation

Last updated: November 2026. The cash-prize wheel and optional contact-entry form are separate features.

1. Free participation

No registration or purchase is required. Each anonymous visitor session is limited to two spins per UTC day across the site. Cookies and network protections are used to limit abuse, but do not guarantee one identity per human. Automated, duplicate or fraudulent participation may be disqualified after review.

2. Prizes and limits

The active pool is configured with up to $5,000 USD in cash prize inventory: $5, $20 and $100 awards. Available inventory decreases as prizes are awarded. The server, not the wheel animation, determines the actual result. See prize rules for current information.

3. Claims and payments

A confirmed winning spin creates a claim linked to the anonymous visitor session. Winners may provide an email address and phone number on the claims page. The admin reviews claims and may send messages to the website inbox. Entering a contact does not independently verify its ownership. An award does not imply an immediate bank transfer; payment arrangements and eligibility must be confirmed by the operator. Never send a fee to claim an award.

4. Optional contact-entry form

The homepage also offers an optional separate entry form collecting an email address and phone number with confirmation consent. Entries are stored through a Cloudflare Worker in its KV storage. Submitting that form does not issue a wheel spin, guarantee any prize, reserve a cash payout, improve winning odds, or create a verified queue rank. A local sharing code is not a guaranteed referral bonus. Entrants should not treat any displayed position or progress animation as a paid-prize entitlement.

5. Advertising

Ads are separate from the prize process. Viewing, clicking or interacting with ads is not required to claim an awarded prize. Advertiser links are third-party destinations.

6. Eligibility and availability

Participation is subject to applicable local laws, age restrictions, eligibility checks and available inventory. A campaign may be paused if operational or compliance concerns arise; existing claims remain recorded. The site operator must provide an accessible support channel before accepting public cash claims.

7. Privacy

Read the privacy notice for details of anonymous session identifiers and claim contact information.
