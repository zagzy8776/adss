Two spins per anonymous browser session per UTC calendar day, shared across all prize campaigns. Clearing browser data or using another device can affect visitor identification. We monitor for abuse; unique-person enforcement is not guaranteed.

Prizes

Claiming

Winning visitors submit an email address and phone number through the claims page. Admin reviews the claim and sends messages to the website inbox. Providing contact details does not automatically verify their ownership or guarantee immediate payment. No account registration is required.

Payment and advertising

Prizes remain pending until reviewed. Payment method and timing are communicated by the operator. You never need to click, watch or interact with advertisements to receive a prize already awarded. No purchase is required to spin.

Availability

The pool stops awarding prizes when available inventory is exhausted or the campaign is paused. Previously awarded claims remain recorded. Local eligibility restrictions and applicable laws may limit participation. Contact the site operator for eligibility and payment questions before participating.
