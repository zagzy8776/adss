Privacy notice

Last updated: November 2026. This notice describes the separate anonymous prize-wheel, optional contact-entry form and advertising features.

What we collect

The site uses an anonymous browser cookie to identify a visitor session and apply a daily spin limit. Server records include spins, prize claims, timestamps, reward balances and anti-abuse events. Winners who choose to submit a claim provide an email address and phone number; these are stored in the Neon PostgreSQL database for administrator review. Separately, the optional homepage entry form collects an email address, phone number, explicit confirmation consent, a referral/share code, entry timestamp and contextual technical information (including visitor device/browser information, language and time zone). That separate form transmits its payload to a Cloudflare Worker and Cloudflare KV store. The wheel visitor cookie is not an identity link to the Cloudflare entry.

Why we use it

To operate the wheel, prevent duplicate claims and abuse, record prize awards, contact winners through the website inbox, review claims, manage optional contact entries, analyze aggregate visit events and maintain administrative audit records. The optional entry form does not grant a cash prize or queue advantage.

Who can access it

Authorized administrators can access claim contact information and optional entry-form data. Hosting, database and advertising providers may process data needed to provide their services. Advertising networks may use cookies or other identifiers under their own privacy policies. The site includes third-party advertising tags; consult the relevant provider policies for their practices.

Contact verification

Submitting an email or phone number does not independently establish ownership. The website inbox is associated with the anonymous browser session, so clearing cookies or switching devices may prevent access to previous claims.

Security and retention

We use server-side access controls for administrative claim information. No online service is completely secure. A verified retention and deletion schedule has not yet been published; the operator must establish one before broader public rollout.

Your choices

You may choose not to submit claim contact information. Browser privacy settings can limit cookies but may affect claim access. A verified contact and privacy-request channel must be published by the operator before launch; no support email address has yet been configured.

Advertising

Ads are not required to obtain an awarded prize. Third-party advertising scripts may have independent tracking and opt-out controls.
