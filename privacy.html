Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026 · Controller: [OPERATOR ENTITY]

1. What we collect

  • Contact details you submit: email address and mobile number, plus your consent record (that you ticked the box, and when).
  • Entry metadata: the queue position shown to you, any referral code in the link you arrived on, the page URL, your time zone, and your browser user-agent string. This is used for fraud prevention and to understand which traffic sources work.
  • Technical data: IP address and approximate city/region derived from it by our hosting provider, used for rate-limiting and for geo-restricting the promotion to the US and Canada.
  • Push subscription: if you enable payout alerts, a browser push token stored by OneSignal. Only created when you click the alerts button.
  • Advertising data: our advertising partners may set cookies or read device identifiers to measure and personalise ads. See section 5.

2. What we do not collect

We never ask for and never store payment card numbers, bank credentials, Cash App/Venmo/PayPal logins, government ID numbers, or gift card codes. Any page that asks you for those while claiming to be us is not us.

3. Why we use it

  • To administer your entry and conduct the draw.
  • To contact you about your entry and your payout — including by SMS if you consented.
  • To detect duplicate, automated, or fraudulent entries.
  • To keep the site working, measure aggregate funnel performance, and serve advertising that funds the pool.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share your email or phone number with advertisers or CPA networks.

5. Who we share it with

  • Hosting: Cloudflare, Inc. serves this site and runs the endpoint that stores entries. Data is processed in the United States.
  • Database: Cloudflare KV, used to store entries and the aggregate payout feed.
  • Push notifications: OneSignal (Lite Push, Inc.) if you enable alerts. It receives a push token and nothing else about you.
  • Advertising: our ad partners (for example Monetag or Adsterra) and, when you click an optional offer, the CPA network behind it. Those companies act as independent controllers under their own privacy policies and may set identifiers to measure advertising. They do not receive your email or phone number from us.
  • Legal: we may disclose information where required by law, subpoena, or to defend legal claims.

6. How long we keep it

Entries are kept for 24 months after the round closes so we can administer payouts, resolve disputes, and meet tax record-keeping obligations, then deleted. Failed submissions buffered on your device are retried for up to 7 days and then dropped. Aggregate counters that contain no personal data are kept indefinitely.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your personal information, to opt out of its sale or sharing, and to not be discriminated against for exercising those rights. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CPRA.

  • US (CCPA/CPRA): request a copy of what we hold, or its deletion, by emailing us. We verify requests against the email address or number on the entry.
  • Canada (PIPEDA): you may request access to your personal information and challenge our compliance with PIPEDA. Quebec residents are excluded from this promotion.
  • Email and SMS: every message we send includes an unsubscribe or STOP instruction. Opting out of marketing does not remove your entry — email us to withdraw it entirely.

To exercise any right, email support@YOURDOMAIN.com with the subject “Privacy request”. We respond within 45 days.

8. Security

Entries are transmitted over TLS and stored in an access-controlled Cloudflare KV namespace. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your information we will notify you as required by applicable law.

9. Children

This site is for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has submitted information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Advertising choices

You can limit interest-based advertising through your device settings (iOS “Allow Apps to Request to Track”, Android “Ads”), or the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out at optout.aboutads.info. Blocking ads does not affect your entry.
