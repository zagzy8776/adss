Winners are identified by first name and last initial only, and only with their consent. Amounts are shown in USD.

How the draw works

  • Each round runs for one calendar month. Entries received during the round are pooled together.
  • At the close of the round, positions are drawn at random from all eligible entries using a verifiable random method. Queue position does not affect your chance of winning.
  • The published pool amount is the total disbursed across all winners in that round, not a single prize.
  • Odds depend entirely on the number of eligible entries received. They are not fixed and are not improved by completing optional offers.

How winners are notified

  • By email to the address on the entry, and by SMS if consent was given.
  • Winners have 72 hours to respond, confirm eligibility, and choose a payout method.
  • If a winner does not respond in time, the entry is disqualified and a replacement is drawn.

Payout methods

Cash App, Venmo, PayPal, or Interac e-Transfer. We never ask a winner for a payment, a deposit, a gift card code, or a fee of any kind. If someone claiming to be from PayoutPool asks you for money, it is not us — report it to support@YOURDOMAIN.com.
