Prize winners

Cash prizes are selected by the live server-backed wheel. This page does not publish personal information or unverified winner announcements.

Check your prize

Won a prize? Visit My prize claims from the same browser, submit your email and phone number, and check messages from the admin there. No account is required.

How it works

Two spins per UTC day are allowed for each anonymous visitor session, subject to abuse controls. The server records the selected prize and its claim. Contact details are submitted directly to the admin for review. See the published rules.

Advertisements are separate from prize claims. Clicking an ad is not required to receive a prize.
