How it works

Are the displayed prizes available now?

No. The current showcase is a preview. Active campaigns must have official rules, eligibility details, dates and verified prize funding.

Does clicking an ad earn cash?

No. Regular advertisements do not generate a personal reward. Only an explicitly authorized, verified rewarded campaign can do so.

How are winners selected?

Each future campaign must publish its selection method, eligibility rules, deadlines and prize-delivery procedure before accepting entries.
